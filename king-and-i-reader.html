The King and I
Or, Why I'm More of a Cat Person.
Story and art by John Staton. An autobiographical short comic about King, the German Shepherd next door, a plastic magenta boomerang that cost $1.32 of lawn-mowing money, and the terrible calculation that went wrong.
“Sorry about my lengthy silence. I have been working on a new, far simpler comic for the dreaded anthology, and have gone on silent running mode, to keep my nose to the grindstone. Fortunately I was given two more weeks to Finnish it, but even with that, I decided it was better to work on a more basic story. Behold, an autobiographical tale of young me as a turtle, retrieving my prized, plastic boomerang from the clutches of the local mean dog. (A little context on why I’m more of a cat person.)”
